What Tincan is

Tincan lets two devices talk to each other directly, encrypted end to end. There is no Tincan server and no account: your identity is a key created on your device. When a direct path is not possible, encrypted traffic goes through a relay, by default the public relays run by Tailscale, or one you set up yourself. What the app does with your data is set out in the privacy policy.

Tincan is free. It has no purchases, no subscriptions and no ads.
