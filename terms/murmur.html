What Murmur is

Murmur plays the music on your device, sends audio and files to other devices on the same network (another Murmur, or a receiver such as Voicemeeter), and, when you turn receiving on, lets those devices play on yours and add to its playlist. It is free. There is no account, no subscription, no advertising and no in-app purchase. Everything happens between devices on your local network; we run no server and see none of it.

Letting friends in

While receiving is on, devices on your network can play on your device and add to its playlist, as far as your access settings allow (Open, Approval or Trust all). Trust all gives anyone on the network full control. Choose the setting that fits the network you are on.

Audio streams and the remote control are not encrypted on the network, so use Murmur on networks you trust. The privacy policy explains what goes where.

Mind the volume, and the road

Sound you send can start at once on a friend’s device, and sound they send can start on yours. Keep the volume at a level that is safe for your hearing and fair to the people around you.

In a car, Murmur’s playlist and library can be controlled through Android Auto. Only handle it when it is safe and legal to do so, and keep your attention on the road.
