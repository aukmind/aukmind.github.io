Services

Fractional CTO
A few days a month of technical leadership: roadmap, architecture, hiring, and an engineer in the room when the board or investors ask hard questions.
Technical due diligence
Before you invest in or acquire a company, we go through the code, the stack and the team, and write down what we find, good and bad.
Consulting
Second opinions on decisions that are expensive to undo: framework and hardware choices, build vs. buy, system architecture.
Product development
Hands-on engineering, written to be handed over. Documented, tested, and not dependent on one person knowing how it works.
Research & benchmarks
Comparing frameworks, AI toolchains or languages with measurements, so the decision isn't made on blog posts.
Training
ROS 2, modern C++, Rust and Python for engineering teams, using your own codebase as the material.

About

Aukmind was founded by Alexis Paques, who has spent 11 years building robots and the software around them, including engineering leadership at Corvus Robotics. The company was incorporated in 2025 to put that work under one roof.

Most projects need more than one kind of expertise. We work with a small network of engineers in embedded systems, electronics, mechanical design, AI and machine learning, data analytics, cloud infrastructure and web development. They join when the work calls for it, and you keep a single point of contact.
